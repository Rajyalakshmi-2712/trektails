About Bhavani Island Resort

Bhavani Island is a breathtaking 133-acre island resort nestled in the Krishna River near Vijayawada, Andhra Pradesh. Managed by the Andhra Pradesh Tourism Development Corporation (APTDC), it is recognized as one of Asia's largest river island resorts. The island offers a rare blend of natural beauty, thrilling adventure activities, and world-class hospitality — all surrounded by the serene waters of the Krishna River.
